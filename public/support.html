Zoomi — Support

How to Use Zoomi

  1. Join a Zoom meeting on the desktop client.
  2. Click the Apps button in the Zoom toolbar.
  3. Find and open Zoomi.
  4. Use the big buttons to send emoji reactions, raise your hand, or mute/unmute your microphone.
  5. Pick a fun theme using the palette button in the top corner.

Troubleshooting

  • Zoomi doesn't appear in Apps: Make sure you're using the latest Zoom desktop client (version 5.11.6 or later).
  • Buttons don't work: Zoomi only works during an active meeting. Make sure you've joined audio in the meeting.
  • Mute button asks for permission: Zoom requires confirmation when an app controls your audio. Click "Allow" to proceed.
