Zoomi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

Overview

Zoomi is a Zoom App that provides kid-friendly meeting controls (emoji reactions, hand raise, and mute/unmute) for educational settings. Your privacy is important to us.

Data We Collect

None. Zoomi does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data. The app runs entirely client-side within the Zoom meeting using the official Zoom Apps SDK.

How Zoomi Works

  • Zoomi uses the Zoom Apps SDK to send emoji reactions, raise/lower hands, and toggle audio mute.
  • All actions happen locally within your Zoom client — no data leaves the Zoom meeting.
  • Zoomi does not have a backend server, database, or analytics service.
  • Zoomi does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of user tracking.

Children's Privacy

Zoomi is designed for use by children ages 5–12 in educational settings such as classrooms and tutoring sessions. Because Zoomi does not collect any data whatsoever, there is no personal information gathered from children or any other users.

Third-Party Services

Zoomi uses Google Fonts (Varela Round) for display purposes. Google Fonts may collect basic usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used.

Data Subject Rights

Since Zoomi does not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, delete, or port. If you have any questions about your data rights, please contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy
